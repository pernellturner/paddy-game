# Paddy's Way Home: the Golden Fury story arc

*Design note (v25). The final God-mode level is **planned, not built yet**.*

## The secret inside Paddy
Paddy is a small ragdoll cat trying to get home to Savannah and Hallie. Every form he finds on the way (Ninja, Samurai, the Legends and the Mythics) is a costume of power he borrows. Deep down there is one power that belongs only to him: **Golden Fury**, the true Paddy. It sleeps until the people he loves need him more than ever.

## Something trying to awaken (during the main game)
A few times on the journey, the God form stirs. Each time is short and a little mysterious, and he can't control it yet:

1. **World 1 (first big boss):** after the win, his fur flickers gold for one second. A low hum and a single heartbeat. Paddy shakes it off: *"…what was that?"*
2. **Mid-game (a Mythic unlock):** the ground cracks in a small ring around his paws and tiny rocks float up, then drop. A faint echo of the announcer whispers *"…rising…"*
3. **Late game (a hard Legend fight):** when Paddy is down to his last life, a golden aura bursts out and pushes every enemy back. The screen shakes and then it's gone. He looks at his paws. The camera lingers.
4. **Before the last boss:** in a dream cut-scene he sees a white-gold cat with a halo floating above a city of light, looking back at him with his own face. *"Not yet,"* it says.

## The last boss, and home
Paddy beats the last boss and finally reaches home. Savannah and Hallie run out and hug him: confetti, his favourite sunny windowsill, the music softens. **Roll a fake "THE END" card.**

## The girls are in trouble (family-friendly)
That night a huge storm of shadow-critters (the scattered minions of every boss, now leaderless and hungry for mischief) sweeps through the neighbourhood. Savannah and Hallie had gone out to the park to bring Paddy's favourite blanket in from the rain. The critter swarm whisks the park away into a floating storm island, carrying the girls off, safe but stranded and scared, inside a glowing bubble.
- No one hurts the girls. They are *lost and trapped*, not harmed. They hold hands, shine a torch and call Paddy's name.
- The threat is the storm and the endless swarm between Paddy and them.

## The true God awakens
Paddy runs out into the rain, but there are thousands of them and only one small cat. He's knocked down and the screen fades to grey. He hears the girls calling his name, and that's enough:
- **Fury 1:** golden fur. **Fury 2:** lightning. **Fury 3:** the great mane. **Fury 4:** crimson fury, cracking the earth.
- **FURY GOD:** the halo forms, the rain stops in mid-air, and the announcer says *"Behold… the Fury God!"*

This is the in-game **⚡ POWER UP** chain, played as one unbroken cinematic.

## The final level: "Bring Them Home" (insane God mode, to be built)
- **Hordes:** waves of hundreds of critters across the sky island, coming from every side. They are cartoony and bounce away in poofs of stars, with no gore.
- **Paddy starts at Fury 1** and powers up as the level goes on, so every level-up is earned, mid-fight.
- **Aerial chaos:** fly between floating islands, juggle the swarm, SPIKE them into craters, SKY SHATTER the big ones into far-off cliffs.
- **Set pieces:** a Fury Cannon across a bridge of critters, a Volcanic Smash that splits the island, and a Divine Judgement that lights the whole storm.
- **The girls:** their bubble drifts across the stage. Protect it, and at the end he flies them home, one under each paw, through the clearing clouds at sunrise.
- **Ending:** back home, the halo fades and he's just Paddy again, asleep on the blanket between Savannah and Hallie. **Real "THE END".**
